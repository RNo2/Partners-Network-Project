The supplied `mocofoodcouncil.org` directory serves Montgomery County **Maryland**, not Pennsylvania. It was investigated and excluded. Share's own find-food page returned 403. Non-Share source labels are not evidence of independence: those providers remain `unknown`, not asserted independent.

Sources may overlap. Source IDs remain stable; exact shared-address candidates are flagged and retained, never fuzzy-merged. Geocoded matches are street-interpolated, not rooftop/survey coordinates. Multiple/failed matches and county conflicts are retained in the inventory but withheld from maps. Retrieval date is not a verification date. Counts of source records are not counts of unique active organizations.

The machine-readable manifest includes retrieval timestamps, raw-content SHA-256 hashes, processed record counts, vintages, coverage and limitations. Raw files are local-only; the deployment includes public processed assets. Full raw ACS response retains every requested estimate and MOE during refresh; the release exposes selected variables' estimates and MOEs and table metadata.

### Newly reconciled supplementary exports

Health of the City is now a separate Philadelphia-only historical context layer, verified against the full official public API. All 47 indicators are inventoried; poverty, unemployment, renter cost burden and population context are prioritized. See [supplementary review](supplied_data_review.md) for vintage, missingness, geography and deduplication details. Regional ACS 2024 and current boundaries are unchanged. The 2010 tract CSV adds reference attributes only; the official Census relationship table is included without invented allocation weights. The meal-site export confirms existing records and clarifies source categories, adding no new providers.
